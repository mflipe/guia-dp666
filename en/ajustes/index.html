DP-666 › The radio

Radio settings

Updated Nov 21, 2026 · 4 sources

Reading version of this page. Search, the tools and the virtual radio are in the interactive guide.

Every menu option on both firmwares, with its possible values, the factory default, what it does and the recommended value for Brazil. On Megatron, many options only show up in advanced mode: see how to turn it on in Setup for Brazil.

This tool works in the interactive guide: open the tool.

Official firmware

Main settings

Model selector

Tells the firmware what type of radio it is running on: desktop (Base), portable with battery (Portable) or portable with a touchscreen (Portable touch).

Default: Portable touch
Values: Base, Portable, Portable touch

Reverse encoder

Reverses the encoder direction: turning right now lowers the frequency, and vice versa.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

Optical encoder

Changes how the encoder pulses are counted to the pattern of optical encoders.

Default: Off
Values: Off, On

Invert colors

Inverts the screen colors, like a photo negative.

Default: Off
Values: Off, On

ARS display

Changes the size, orientation and color order of the screen to suit the panel of another build (ARS).

Default: Off
Values: Off, On

Analog meter

Puts the needle of an analog signal meter at the end of the scale, so you can adjust it.

Values: Puts the needle at full scale, for adjustment.

Factory reset

Returns every setting to the factory default and erases the 99 memory channels. The radio asks for confirmation.

Values: Asks for confirmation: “Erase settings and presets?”

Stop screen wake on tune

With the screensaver on, turning the knob now tunes without waking the screen. When off, the first turn only wakes the screen.

Default: Off
Values: Off, On

Auto Power off

Puts the radio on standby after 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes without you touching anything.

Default: Off
Values: Off, 15 min, 30 min, 60 min, 90 min

Use squelch

The squelch mutes the audio when there is no useful signal, so you don’t hear hiss between stations. On uses the radio’s SQL knob. Auto decides on its own from the signal quality.

Default: On
In Brazil: Off. To hunt for weak stations. On Auto, the radio may hide distant signals.
Values: On, Off, Auto

FM AGC

Sets the signal strength at which the radio starts lowering its own amplification on FM.

Default: 92 dBµV
In Brazil: 92 dBµV. It is the most sensitive. If ghost stations show up near strong stations, try 87 or 84.
Values: 84 to 92 dBµV

AM AGC

The same as FM AGC, on AM and shortwave: the signal strength at which the radio lowers its own amplification.

Default: 100 dBµV
Values: 94 to 102 dBµV

Select FM Band

Chooses which FM bands the BAND button cycles through: FM, OIRT or both.

Default: FM, OIRT
Values: FM, OIRT, OIRT, FM, None

Select AM Band

Chooses which AM bands the BAND button cycles through: LW (long wave), MW (medium wave) and SW (shortwave).

Default: LW, MW, SW
Values: LW, MW, SW, LW, MW, LW, SW, MW, SW, LW, MW, SW, None

5 sec. bandbutton press

Sets what happens when you hold BAND: put the radio on standby (Power off), turn off only the screen (Screen off) or nothing (Disabled).

Default: Power off
Values: Power off, Screen off, Disabled

Dynamic SPI speed

Changes the speed of the link between the processor and the screen (10 to 60 MHz). On Auto, the radio picks the speed according to the tuned frequency.

Default: Auto
Values: Default, 10 MHz, 20 MHz, 30 MHz, 40 MHz, 50 MHz, 60 MHz, Auto

Audio settings

Set volume

Internal audio gain of the tuner (from −10 to +10 dB), applied before the volume knob.

Default: 0 dB
Values: −10 to 10 dB

Audio feedback

Turns on navigation sounds: beeps in the menu, at the end of the band and when saving to the logbook. With On + morse, numbers and letters also play in Morse code.

Default: Off
Values: Off, On, On + morse

Audio output

In MPX mode, the left channel is mono and the right one carries the station’s full signal, with the stereo pilot and RDS.

Default: Stereo
Values: Stereo, MPX

Stereo separation threshold

Below this signal strength, the radio reduces stereo separation and moves toward mono.

Default: Off
In Brazil: ≈ 40 dBµV. With a weak signal, it forces mono and reduces hiss.
Values: Off or 30 to 60 dBµV

High cut corner frequency

When the treble cut kicks in, it attenuates the sound above this frequency.

Default: 7000 Hz
Values: 1500 to 7000 Hz

High cut threshold

Below this signal strength, the treble cut kicks in.

Default: Off
Values: Off or 20 to 60 dBµV

FM deemphasis

The station boosts the treble when transmitting (pre-emphasis), and the radio cuts it by the same amount when receiving. The sound returns to natural and the hiss drops.

Default: 50 µs
In Brazil: 50 µs. Brazil uses 50 µs, as required by Anatel Act No. 8,104/2022, unlike the rest of the Americas.
Values: Off, 50 µs, 75 µs

Language

Language of the menus, among 23.

Default: English
Values: English, Nederlands, Polski, Hrvatski, Ελληνικά, Română, Deutsch, Čeština, Magyar, Français, Български, Русский, Українська, Italiano, Simplified Chinese, Norsk, Español, English, Srpski, Suomi, Dansk, Svenska, Gaeilge

Default: 50%
Values: 1 to 100 %

Default: On
Values: Off, On

Screensaver

After 3, 10, 30 or 60 seconds without use, the screen turns off or dims.

Default: Off
Values: Off, 3 s, 10 s, 30 s, 60 s

Screensaver options

Sets what the screensaver does: turn off the screen or leave it at 1%, 25% or 50% brightness.

Default: 1% brightness
Values: Screen off, 1% brightness, 25% brightness, 50% brightness

Battery options

Shows the battery in volts, as a percentage, or not at all.

Default: Show voltage
Values: Off, Show voltage, Show percentage

Signalmeter unit

Unit in which the radio shows the signal strength.

Default: dBµV
Values: dBµV, dBf, dBm

Show clock

Shows the time on the screen when the radio has the right time, received through RDS or the internet.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

Theme

Color scheme of the interface. There are 17 themes.

Default: Essence
Values: Essence, Cyan, Crimson, Monochrome, Volcano, Dendro, Sakura, Whiteout, Tangerine, Ocean, Indigo, Queer, GoldBrite, Bubblegum, Aurora, Ember, Glacier

Frequency font

Font of the large frequency digits.

Default: Aura2
Values: Classic, Roubenstil, Motoya, Aura2, Modern

Flip screen 180°

Turns the screen image upside down.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

Frequency keypad

Turns on the on-screen numeric keypad, to enter the frequency.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

Touch calibration

Opens the touch calibration: you tap the points shown on the screen.

Values: Asks for confirmation: “Start calibration?”

RDS settings

Show RDS errors

Sets how many errors the radio accepts in the RDS data: Off accepts only perfect blocks, Small accepts small corrected errors and Large accepts even large corrected errors.

Default: Small
Values: Off, Small, Large

Region

Adapts RDS to the region: the program type (PTY) table and, in the United States and Canada, the conversion of the station code into a call sign, such as KXYZ.

Default: Europe
In Brazil: Europe. Brazilian RDS follows the European model, with PI codes assigned by Anatel.
Values: Europe, USA DB, USA, Canada

Show underscore in RDS

Replaces spaces with underscores: only in the station name (PS only) or also in the text (On).

Default: Off
Values: Off, PS only, On

RDS filter

Runs the RDS data through a filter with memory before showing it.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

Show PI errors

Shows the station’s PI code even before it arrives without errors.

Default: Off
Values: Off, On

Use AF

With AF on, the radio switches on its own to another frequency of the same station when the signal gets worse. With REG on, it only accepts frequencies with the same regional programming.

Default: Off
In Brazil: Off. For DX and scanning, it keeps the radio from jumping to another frequency on its own.
Values: Off, On / REG on, On / REG off

RT buffering

Only shows the RadioText, the text that scrolls on the screen, once it arrives complete, instead of changing it letter by letter.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

Auto sort AF

Sorts the list of alternative frequencies (AF) from lowest to highest.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

Fast PS

Shows the letters of the station name (PS) as soon as they arrive, without waiting for confirmation. Initial does this only until the name is complete; Always, all the time.

Default: Initial
Values: Off, Initial, Always

Show long PS

Shows the station’s long name (Long PS), up to 32 characters, when it broadcasts one.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

FM settings

Converter offset

Adds a value to the displayed frequency, for those using an external RF converter.

Default: 0 MHz
Values: 0 to 2400 MHz

Low band edge

Lowest frequency of the FM band. Past it, the knob wraps around to the end of the band.

Default: 87.5 MHz
In Brazil: 76.1 MHz. The band now covers extended FM, from 76.1 to 87.3 MHz.
Values: 65 to 107.9 MHz

High band edge

Highest frequency of the FM band. Past it, the knob wraps around to the low edge.

Default: 108.0 MHz
In Brazil: 108.0 MHz. The band now covers extended FM, from 76.1 to 87.3 MHz.
Values: 66 to 108 MHz

Level offset

Adds or subtracts decibels from the signal reading.

Default: 0 dB
Values: −25 to 15 dB

Low level threshold

Below this signal strength, the radio grays out the modulation meter and the RDS labels.

Default: −15 dBµV
Values: −15 to 30 dBµV

Softmute FM

Gradually lowers the volume as the signal weakens and the hiss rises.

Default: Off
Values: Off, On

FM Noise blanker

Cuts impulse noise: the short clicks from motors, electric fences and electrical appliances. The percentage is the strength of the cut.

Default: Off
Values: Off or 50 to 150 %

FM default stepsize

How far the frequency moves with each click of the knob on FM.

Default: 100 kHz
In Brazil: 200 kHz. With the edge at 76.1 MHz, tuning steps through the Brazilian channels, which end in an odd decimal. For DX, use 100 kHz to also stop on the even decimals.
Values: 50 kHz, 100 kHz, 200 kHz

FMSI stereo improvement

Turns on processing that improves stereo on weak signals. It only exists on the TEF6687 and TEF6689 tuners.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

Scan sensitivity

How demanding the search is before stopping on a FM station, from 1 to 30.

Default: 4
Values: 1 to 30

AM settings

Softmute AM

Gradually lowers the volume as the AM signal weakens and the noise rises.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

AM Noise blanker

Cuts impulse noise: the short clicks from motors, electric fences and electrical appliances. The percentage is the strength of the cut.

Default: Off
Values: Off or 50 to 150 %

AM RF Level offset

Adds or subtracts decibels from the signal reading on AM.

Default: 0 dB
Values: −50 to 15 dB

Show SW wavelength

Shows which shortwave band you are in, in meters, such as 49 m or 31 m, next to the frequency.

Default: On
Values: Off, On

CoChannel Detector

Turns on the co-channel detector: the radio shows CO on the screen when it notices two stations on the same frequency. The number is the sensitivity.

Default: 100%
Values: Off or 50 to 150 %

CoChannel Detector threshold

How many detections in a row the co-channel detector needs before showing CO.

Default: 3
Values: 1 to 15

AM Antenna gain

Attenuates the AM input by 6 to 36 dB. The name talks about gain because the idea is that you enter the gain of your active antenna, and the radio attenuates by the same amount.

Default: Off
Values: Off or 6 to 36 dB

MW default stepsize

How far the frequency moves with each click on medium wave: 9 or 10 kHz.

Default: 9 kHz
In Brazil: 10 kHz. It is the medium wave grid of the Americas, from 520 to 1720 kHz.
Values: 9 kHz, 10 kHz

Scan sensitivity

How demanding the search is before stopping on a AM station, from 1 to 30.

Default: 4
Values: 1 to 30

Turns on the radio’s Wi-Fi.

Values: Off, On

Configure Wi-Fi

Opens the wizard that connects the radio to a network.

Values: Up to 3 networks, 2.4 GHz only: with the on-screen keypad (SSID and password), with a browser (portal at 192.168.4.1) or Delete Wi-Fi.

Set XDRGTK password

Password XDR-GTK needs to control the radio over the network.

Values: Up to 8 characters.

Stationlist client IP

IP address of the computer running StationList.

Values: IP address.

Stationlist ID

This radio’s number in StationList.

Values: 1 to 10

Screen off on XDRGTK

Turns the radio’s screen off while XDR-GTK is connected.

Values: Off, On

Wi-Fi auto disconnect

Turns Wi-Fi off after the chosen time, or right after setting the clock (After time sync).

In Brazil: After time sync. Wi-Fi turns off after setting the clock, which saves battery and keeps the battery reading working.
Values: From 1 to 1440 min, or After time sync

Set NTP server

Internet time server. It comes set to cn.pool.ntp.org, in China.

Default: cn.pool.ntp.org
In Brazil: a.st1.ntp.br. It is the NTP.br server, which uses the atomic clocks of Brazil’s National Observatory.
Values: Server address.

Bluetooth

Turns on Bluetooth so the radio can control another device (Config slave host).

Values: Config slave host: control of another device, without audio.

Touch type

How the firmware reads the touchscreen: by interrupt (Int33 or Int27) or by periodic polling (Polling). Off disables touch.

Values: Int33, Int27, Off, Polling

Touch calibration

Opens the touch calibration: you tap the points shown on the screen.

Values: Opens the calibration.

Rotary adjust squelch

Lets you adjust the squelch threshold by turning the tuning knob, in a mode you turn on and off.

Values: Off, On

Knob mode

Knob operating mode: Classic or New.

Values: Classic, New

Knob reverse

Reverses the knob direction: turning right now lowers the frequency, and vice versa.

Values: Off, On

Knob acceleration

Speeds up tuning when you turn the knob fast: Medium or Fast.

Values: Off, Medium, Fast

Quickly switch menu

Shortcut to switch menu groups in fewer steps.

Values: Off, On

Chooses what a long press on this key does: lock the keys, the original function, open the menu, the keypad, the shortcuts page, advanced RDS or the AF screen.

Values: Lock&Unlock, Original operation, Open menu, Digital keypad, Shortcut page, Advanced RDS, AF Screen

Long press BW key

Chooses what a long press on this key does: lock the keys, the original function, open the menu, the keypad, the shortcuts page, advanced RDS or the AF screen.

Values: Lock&Unlock, Original operation, Open menu, Digital keypad, Shortcut page, Advanced RDS, AF Screen

Long press MODE key

Chooses what a long press on this key does: lock the keys, the original function, open the menu, the keypad, the shortcuts page, advanced RDS or the AF screen.

Values: Lock&Unlock, Original operation, Open menu, Digital keypad, Shortcut page, Advanced RDS, AF Screen

Long press BAND key

Chooses what a long press on this key does: lock the keys, the original function, open the menu, the keypad, the shortcuts page, advanced RDS or the AF screen.

Values: Lock&Unlock, Original operation, Open menu, Digital keypad, Shortcut page, Advanced RDS, AF Screen

Auto save change

Saves menu changes on its own. With Off, the radio asks Save or Discard when you exit.

Values: Off, On

Other settings

Many of these options only appear in advanced mode.

Set date and time

Sets the date and time by hand.

Values: Date, time and date + time mode.

12/24 h

Shows the clock in 12 h or 24 h format.

Values: 12 h, 24 h

Set local time zone

Time zone applied to the time coming from the internet, as GMT±hh:mm.

In Brazil: GMT−03:00. Brasília time. Pick the time zone of your state.
Values: Time zone as GMT±hh:mm.

NTP time sync

Sets the clock over the internet when Wi-Fi is on; since Mod16, every 30 minutes.

Values: Off, On

RDS time sync

Sets the clock from the time the station sends through RDS.

In Brazil: Off. With NTP working, because some stations send the wrong time.
Values: Off, On

Sleep clock correction

Corrects the clock by a few seconds every 24 hours.

Values: Seconds per 24 h.

Power on timer

Turns the radio on by itself at a set time.

Values: Time.

Low battery protect

Turns the radio off when the battery reaches the chosen limit.

Values: Battery limit.

Delete RDS dictionary

Erases the RDS dictionary stored by the radio.

Values: Shows Deleted or Not deleted.

System information

Shows the firmware version, the build date, memory, storage, the tuner, the time server, the IP address and the MAC address.

Values: Version, build date, RAM, storage, tuner, NTP, IP and MAC.

Developer mode e Manage my data

Development and data management functions.

Values: Not documented.

My settings

ATS / Scan schemes

Stores auto search (ATS) and scan schemes, with band and criteria.

Values: Saved schemes.

My theme

Switches the theme on its own among up to 4 favorites, in sequence or at random.

Values: Sequential, Random

Receive plan

Turns the radio on and off on a schedule, on a set band and frequency.

Values: Band, frequency and time.

My Shortcut icon

Chooses the 7 shortcuts on the shortcuts page.

Values: 7 shortcuts.

Save / Delete my settings

Saves your settings as a personal factory default, or deletes that saved set.

Values: Saves or erases the personal set.

Auto search

Search & save mode

How auto search saves: Overwrite erases the band’s memory channels and saves again; Append adds after the existing ones.

Values: Overwrite, Append, Continuously append

Search band

Band for the auto search.

Values: FM, LW, MW, SW, AIR and others; the manual’s list goes on

FM scan sensitivity

How demanding the search is before stopping on a FM station, from 1 to 15.

Values: 1 to 15

AM scan sensitivity

How demanding the search is before stopping on a AM station, from 1 to 20.

Values: 1 to 20

Judgment mode

Auto search criterion: Quality priority only saves good signals; Quantity priority saves as many stations as possible.

Values: Quality priority, Quantity priority

Start channel

First memory channel the auto search uses.

Values: 1 to 99

SW search start / stop

Shortwave search limits, from 1800 to 27000 kHz.

Values: 1800 to 27000 kHz

Arrange stations

Reorganizes the memory channels: only removes the gaps (Only defrag) or removes them and sorts by frequency (Defrag and sort).

Values: Only defrag, Defrag and sort

Delete stations

Erases all memory channels of the chosen band, not just one.

Values: Empties the whole chosen band.

Frequency scanning

Scanning band

DX scan band.

Values: FM, LW, MW, SW, AIR, Default, FML

Begin / End channel

First and last memory channel of the scan.

Values: Values not documented.

Wait time

Time on each frequency during the scan.

Values: Values not documented.

Exclude / Only

With Only, the scan goes only through the memory channels of the range. With Exclude, it scans the whole band and skips the frequencies saved in that range.

Values: Exclude, Only

Cancel

Makes the scan stop on its own: when it decodes a station’s code (Correct PI) or when it finds a signal (Signal). Off never stops.

Values: Signal, Off, Correct PI

Stay / Break

What the scan does when it stops on a station: stays on it (Stay) or ends (Break).

Values: Stay, Break

Wait on signal only

Uses the wait time only on frequencies with a signal; on empty ones, the scan moves on right away.

Values: Off, On

Hold time

Scan hold time.

Values: Values not documented.
